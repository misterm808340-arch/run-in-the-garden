Avoid
Rocks, pots, branches, bushes, puddles and tall trellises. Low ones can be jumped; tall ones must be dodged. A crash ends the run!
Power-ups
Shield - blocks one crash
Magnet - pulls nearby coins
Speed Boost - dash & smash obstacles
Double Coins - 2x coins for a while
Score
Score = distance + coins (coin 10, star 50). Beat your best!
About Ads
This build shows Google AdMob test ads: a banner on menus, an occasional interstitial between runs, and optional rewarded videos (continue after a crash, double your coins).
