Privacy Policy

Run in the Garden · Effective date: September 20, 2026 · Version 1.0

This Privacy Policy explains how Run in the Garden (the "Game") handles information when you play it on your device, whether as a web app or as an installed Android application. We have designed the Game to work fully offline, and we collect as little information as possible.

1. Information We Collect

The Game does not require an account, does not use cloud authentication, and does not upload your gameplay data to any server operated by us. There is no backend server receiving your scores or settings.

Local data stored on your device only:

  • Best score, best distance and total coins collected;
  • Number of games played;
  • Audio settings (music and sound effects on/off).

This data is saved in your browser's or device's local storage and never leaves your device. Clearing the app data or the browser storage removes it permanently.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game displays advertisements through Google AdMob, which is currently configured in test mode (Google's official test ad units). AdMob is provided by Google LLC and, when advertising is active in a release build, Google may process certain information such as device identifiers, approximate location (at the country/city level), and advertising interaction data to serve and measure ads.

About test mode: while the Game uses AdMob test ads, no real advertising revenue or user profiling takes place. Before a production release, the ad unit IDs are replaced and Google's policies (including any user-consent requirements such as the Google User Messaging Platform) apply.

For more information about how Google uses data, see Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy. Where required by law, a consent prompt (for example, from Google's UMP SDK) will be shown before personalized ads are served.

3. Children's Privacy

The Game is a casual runner suitable for a general audience. The advertising configuration is requested with child-directed treatment settings enabled and a "General" maximum content rating. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us and we will address it.

4. Permissions

The Game does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location or files. As an installed app it only needs storage access to keep your local progress, which is handled by the operating system automatically.

5. Offline Operation

All game assets, code, sounds and levels are bundled with the Game. No online API is called during gameplay, and the Game remains fully playable in airplane mode. The service worker (in the web version) only caches Game files on your own device to enable offline play.

6. Data Security and Retention

Because all gameplay data stays on your device, no data is transmitted over the network by us. Your local progress is retained until you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when advertising configuration changes. The "effective date" above shows the current version. Continued use of the Game after an update means you accept the revised policy.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to: support@gardenrun.example.com (replace with your support address before publishing).
